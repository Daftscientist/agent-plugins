# Imagery

## Photographs

Crop to support the focal point, not just to fit the frame. Preserve aspect ratio — never stretch. Decide full-bleed vs. contained deliberately per composition (see `references/compositions.md`). Use images of sufficiently high quality that they hold up at presentation scale. When text overlays a photo, check contrast against the actual busiest region of the image, not just its average tone.

## Screenshots

Preserve UI readability — a screenshot shrunk until its text is illegible has stopped being evidence. Crop out irrelevant chrome (browser tabs, unrelated UI, notifications). Never stretch a screenshot to fill a frame it doesn't match. Use a frame or background deliberately, as part of the chosen treatment, not automatically. Zoom into the specific region that matters rather than showing a whole screen for one relevant detail.

## Logos

Preserve aspect ratio always. Give logos breathing room — a logo touching its container edge reads as careless. Avoid recoloring a logo arbitrarily unless the brand/user explicitly permits it. Use vector source where available rather than a rasterized logo that will look soft at scale.

## Diagrams and technical images

Fit the frame to the diagram rather than cropping away content that changes its meaning. Prefer annotating a diagram over shrinking it until it's unreadable — if it needs to be small, simplify it first.

## Treatments

A treatment is a consistent way of presenting imagery across the deck — pick one (or a small deliberate set) and hold it:

```text
full bleed            image fills the entire slide
editorial crop         a considered, magazine-style crop with strong negative space
cutout subject          subject isolated from its background
duotone                 image mapped to two colors
monochrome              image desaturated to grayscale
tinted                  a single color wash over the image
masked geometric crop   image clipped to a non-rectangular shape
collage                 multiple images composed together
framed screenshot        screenshot inside a device/browser chrome frame
floating screenshot      screenshot with a drop shadow, not embedded in a frame
contact-sheet / gallery  a grid of related images shown together
documentary              unstaged, real-world photography
cinematic                 wide aspect, dramatic lighting/mood
split-tone                 shadows and highlights tinted with two different colors
grain overlay               subtle film-grain texture added for warmth/analog feel
vignette                    edges gently darkened to pull focus toward the center
line-art overlay             a thin illustrated line layer added over a photo
color-block backdrop         image placed against a solid color field rather than full bleed
repeating grid mosaic         many small consistent-crop images arranged in a uniform grid
```

Consistency matters more than any individual treatment: a deck that mixes full-bleed documentary photos with framed cutout screenshots and duotone icons has no coherent imagery language, even if each individual choice is defensible in isolation.
